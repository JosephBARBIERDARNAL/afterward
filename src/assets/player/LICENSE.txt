The player's sprite sheets in `sheets/`, and the loose effect sheets in
`../effects/`, are cut from three free Craftpix "prototype character" packs:

  - Free Pixel Art Prototype Character Sprites
  - Free 2D Pixel Prototype Character Pack 2
  - Prototype Pixel Hero Free Sprite Pack 3

License: https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/

Each file here is one animation from those packs, renamed to the clip it
plays (see `animations.yaml`). The packs' own folders, their PSD sources and
the animations the game decided against are not kept in the repository.

The sheets in `sheets/` are no longer the packs' own files: each was written
out as Typst text art in `art/player/sheets/`, which is now their source, and
the PNGs here are rendered from it (`just art`).
